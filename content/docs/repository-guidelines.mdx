---

![Spot-checking AI agents at the merge gate](/images/merge-gate-agents.jpg)

Every repository onboarded in CodeOtter is checked at its root, through its forge API, for `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Zero configuration**: when either file (or both) exists, its guidelines are added to the review context.
- **Enforced at the merge gate**: violations of architecture conventions, file structure or coding guidelines fail the `Repository guidelines` gate.
- **Per-repository control**: turn guideline enforcement on or off per repository with **Repository settings → Review guidelines**.

Guidelines apply to reviews, PR tools and the [CLI](/docs/cli). Combine them with [team learnings](/docs/review-features#team-learnings-and-accepted-fixes) to capture rules that are not written down yet.
